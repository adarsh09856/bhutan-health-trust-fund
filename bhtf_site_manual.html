Platform Architecture & Executive Overview

TanStack Start · React 19 · Vite · Drizzle ORM · PostgreSQL

The Bhutan Health Trust Fund (BHTF) platform is the official apex digital sovereign repository for primary healthcare endowment financing, essential medicines security, universal routine immunization, and cold-chain resilience across the Kingdom of Bhutan. Established pursuant to the Royal Charter granted by His Majesty the Fourth Druk Gyalpo, it sustainably protects all 780,000+ citizens across 205 Gewogs in all 20 Dzongkhags.

Core Architecture & Technical Capabilities

  1. Full-Stack Architecture: Built with TanStack Start (full-stack React with Server-Side Rendering), TanStack Router, Tailwind CSS v4, and Drizzle ORM on PostgreSQL. Guarantees instantaneous first-contentful paint, zero cumulative layout shift, and complete search engine indexation.
  2. Zero-Lag Live Synchronization: Content updates made in administrative studios write directly to the database and revalidate server endpoints. Public pages update in real-time without caching lag or manual deployments.
  3. 5-Tier Admin Suite: Organized into five distinct operational divisions (Executive CRM & Donors, Commodities & Procurement, Institutional CMS & Media, Sovereign Governance, and System Administration) with strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
  4. Editorial Floating Glass Navigation Island: An adaptive glassmorphic capsule that elevates seamlessly on scroll (backdrop-blur-2xl), auto-adjusts its top offset when an administrator is logged in, and delivers streamlined dropdown menus for About Us, Our Impact, and Resources.
  5. Verified Sovereign Fiduciary Ledger: Full reconciliation workflow for online payments (RMA BFS, Razorpay) and direct bank deposits (Bank of Bhutan, Bhutan National Bank, Druk PNB) with automated DRC 100% Tax Exemption certificate issuance.
⚡ Public Reflection Architecture
All database writes execute with atomic transactions. When an administrative record is saved (e.g. an offline pledge is reconciled or an annual RAA audit report is uploaded), the corresponding public endpoints re-render live data across all public routes immediately.

Admin Control Center & Navigation Layout

/admin · /admin/login

Staff Authentication & Security Guard

  1. Open your browser and navigate to /admin/login.
  2. Enter your authorized administrative email (e.g. admin@bhtf.bt) and secure password.
  3. Click Authenticate & Access Studio.
  4. Upon bcrypt-verified credential match, the server generates an encrypted session token and redirects to the Executive CRM Dashboard (/admin/dashboard).

The 5 Administrative Divisions

All administrative studios are structured within a standardized sidebar hierarchy:

  • 1. Executive CRM & Donors: Dashboard Overview, Donors & Pledges CRM, Citizen Inquiries & Ombudsman Desk, Subscribers & Audiences.
  • 2. Commodities & Procurement: Essential Medicines & 6 Streams, Procurement Tenders & Steps, National Impact Statistics.
  • 3. Institutional CMS & Media: Pages & Templates, Live Page Studio, News & Press Releases, Reports & RAA Audits, Policies & Royal Charters, Field Operations Gallery, Public Media & Videos.
  • 4. Sovereign Governance: Board of Trustees Directory, Historical Milestones Timeline, FAQs & Citizen Helpdesk.
  • 5. System Administration: Users & Roles (RBAC), Security Audit Trail, Sovereign Site Settings.

Visual Live Edit Mode & Front-End Studio

Admin Live Bar · In-Context Front-End Customizer

Visual Live Edit Mode enables secretariat editors to browse public pages and make real-time updates directly on the front-end layout without navigating complex back-office database tables.

Using the Sticky Admin TopBar

  1. Log into the Admin Panel, then browse any public page (e.g. /, /about, /our-story, /our-work, /reports).
  2. A dark Sovereign Admin Bar appears pinned at the top of your screen showing your active slug.

Studio Controls & Responsive Viewports

  • Responsive Device Switcher: Toggle between Desktop (100%), Tablet (768px), and Mobile (375px) viewports to verify layouts across all screen form factors.
  • Block Reordering: Drag section blocks up or down to modify the visual hierarchy.
  • Section Visibility Toggle: Eye icon enables toggling blocks between published and hidden states.
⚡ Public Reflection Point
Clicking Save & Publish Live in the studio writes directly to the custom_pages schema. The public route instantly updates to render the modified section layout and copy.

Donors & Pledges CRM & DRC Tax Verification

/admin/donations · /track-donation

Administers all public and corporate contributions made via RMA BFS gateway, Razorpay, or direct Bank of Bhutan / Druk PNB / BNB bank transfers.

Verifying Offline Bank Transfers

  1. Navigate to Executive CRM & Donors → Donors & Pledges CRM.
  2. Filter transactions by Pending Verification.
  3. Click on the donor's transaction entry to inspect the payment slip, bank journal reference number, and donor citizen identity details.
  4. Cross-verify the payment with the Secretariat's Bank of Bhutan (BoB) deposit ledger.
  5. Click Approve & Reconcile Donation.
⚡ Public Reflection Points
When a contribution status transitions to Verified:
1. Corpus Accumulation: The public counter and Sovereign Health Corpus Card on the homepage automatically include the new verified amount.
2. Instant Certificate Generation: The donor can immediately enter their Journal/Reference ID on /track-donation to download their official sealed Department of Revenue & Customs (DRC) 100% Tax Exemption Certificate.

Essential Medicines & 6 Commodity Procurement Streams

/admin/programs · /our-work

Maintains the national essential health formulary and funding allocations across Bhutan's six statutory commodity streams.

The 6 Statutory Streams

  • Stream 1: Universal Childhood & Maternal Vaccines (14 National Antigens).
  • Stream 2: 120+ Vital Essential Medicines Formulary (Zero stockout buffer guarantee).
  • Stream 3: Alpine Solar Cold Chain Logistics (Solar refrigeration & temperature monitoring).
  • Stream 4: Primary Diagnostic Reagents & Screenings (Blood glucose, Hepatitis, Pap/HPV).
  • Stream 5: Clean Delivery Kits & Maternal Survival Packs (Remote obstetric health).
  • Stream 6: Emergency Disaster Stockpile & Infusion Reserves (Critical IV fluids & trauma buffer).

Editing a Commodity Stream

  1. Navigate to Commodities & Procurement → Essential Medicines & Streams.
  2. Click Edit Stream on any commodity card.
  3. Update allocated funds (e.g. Nu. 68,500,000), beneficiaries count, or gewog coverage metrics.
  4. Click Save Stream Changes.
⚡ Public Reflection Points
Modifications made here reflect immediately on:
1. /our-work — full interactive commodity formulary cards and supply breakdowns.
2. / (Homepage) — Commodity Tracker pipeline and Hero Corpus Card supply allocation rows.

Security Audit Trail & Fiduciary Compliance Logs

/admin/audit-logs

An immutable, tamper-evident security ledger recording every administrative action taken inside the system.

Audit Ledger Contents

Every log entry captures:

  • Timestamp (ISO 8601 local Bhutan time).
  • Staff Email & User ID who executed the action.
  • Action Category (e.g. DONATION_VERIFIED, TENDER_PUBLISHED, PAGE_SECTION_SAVED, BANK_ACCOUNT_MODIFIED).
  • Client IP Address and browser user agent.
  • Old vs New State Payload (JSON diff).
The audit trail cannot be edited or cleared by any administrator. It is exported annually for inspection by the Royal Audit Authority (RAA) statutory auditors.

Disaster Recovery, Database Backups & VPS Maintenance

CLI · Nitro · PM2 · PostgreSQL Dump

Automated Database Backups

The database automatically backs up daily to encrypted storage. To run a manual on-demand backup on the production VPS server:

npm run db:backup

Backup archives are timestamped and stored in .backups/ with full PostgreSQL dump files.

Zero-Downtime Deployment

To deploy new system updates without interrupting citizen services:

git pull origin main
npm run build
pm2 reload bhtf-server || pm2 restart bhtf-server
DevOps Security Advisory: Database credentials and server SSH keys are confidential sovereign assets. Store all environment variables securely and never commit .env files to public repositories.
