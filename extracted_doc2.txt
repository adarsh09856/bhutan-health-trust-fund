BHUTAN HEALTH TRUST FUNDWEBSITE REMARKS, CHANGES & ACTION PLAN
Consolidated requirements, final navigation structure and reference websites
1. General Design & Content
Keep the website clean, professional, natural, consistent and easy to navigate.
Maintain uniformity in wording, terminology, headings, fonts, spacing, image sizing and presentation.
Do not unnecessarily change information already provided and approved.
Avoid too many main menu items; group related information logically.
Make frequently updated sections dynamic/editable.
Use high-resolution, natural-looking, relevant photographs; source suitable high-quality images online when needed.
Avoid overly artificial or AI-looking photographs.
Keep content concise and visually organized rather than using large blocks of text.

Donation Messages & Content
Use concise, meaningful messages.
“Your gift protects the health of every Bhutanese forever.”
“No Buddhist should ever have to choose between their health and their livelihood.”
Present these messages visually and avoid making the page text-heavy.
Tax Exemption
Include a concise explanation of applicable tax exemption.
Refer to relevant Department of Revenue and Customs (DRC) guidance.
Possible caption: “Give generously, save on tax.”
Use the officially applicable percentage/rule and keep it editable if requirements change.
One-to-One Golden Matching
Include a concise explanation of the initiative and how matching works, using officially approved wording.
Capital Preservation
Explain the capital-preservation concept simply.
The intended explanation is that the principal donation is preserved while the applicable return/interest supports the intended purpose.
Example discussed: a Nu. 500 contribution preserves the principal while applicable interest/return is utilized according to the established mechanism.
Confirm final financial wording before publication.
Return on Investment
Include a short, clear explanation of the applicable return/interest associated with the donation mechanism.
Dedication
Allow donations for birthdays, celebrations, in memory of someone, and other meaningful occasions.

20. Reference Websites / Design Benchmarks
Use the following Bhutanese organizations as reference points for structure, content presentation, navigation, governance/institutional information, resources, stories/impact, news, and donation/get-involved presentation. These are references only; do not copy their branding or content.
Bhutan Trust Fund for Environmental Conservation
https://www.bhutantrustfund.bt
Useful reference for trust-fund institutional presentation, governance, organizational information, announcements and downloadable reports. The current site presents About Us information, governance and News & Stories.
Bhutan Kidney Foundation
https://bkfbhutan.org
Useful reference for About Us, programmes/services, News & Announcements, Downloads, Reports, donation/get-involved options, and success-story/impact presentation.
Bhutan Toilet Organization
https://www.bhutantoilet.org
Useful reference for organizational storytelling, history/milestones, activities/projects, campaigns, impact and public-facing presentation.
21. Final Action Checklist
Keep the top navigation limited to the six approved main items.
Keep Our Story and Our Impact separate.
Move Window Financing under Resources.
Keep News and Events separate categories under one News & Events menu.
Place Activities under Events or in the footer; do not create a separate main menu item.
Provide Domestic and International donation options.
Add domestic QR/payment information.
Include tax exemption, Golden Matching, capital preservation, return/interest and dedication information.
Use existing approved photographs where applicable and update only those that need changing.
Maintain uniformity in wording, photographs, layout and presentation.
Make frequently changing sections dynamic/editable.
Make relevant official resources and historical records publicly accessible/downloadable.
Use the three reference websites as design/content benchmarks without copying their branding or content.
22. Overall Objective
The website should be simple, professional, consistent, transparent, accountable, easy to navigate and easy to maintain. Related information should be grouped together; the main navigation should not be overcrowded; Our Story and Our Impact should remain separate; Window Financing should remain under Resources; and frequently changing information should be dynamic/editable.
